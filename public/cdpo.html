DAY 1: ORIENTATION & ACT MAP

Orientation and Act Map (Section 1)

Welcome to Day 1 of the official Certified Data Protection Officer (CDPO) study programme. This session grounds your understanding of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 structure, purpose, and 12 statutory parts.

Key Statutory Purpose

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 was signed into law on June 12, 2023. It establishes an independent data protection authority (the Nigeria Data Protection Commission), guarantees the rights of data subjects, and creates strict compliance and governance mandates for data controllers and processors across all sectors.

STATUTORY PROVISION (SECTION 1) "The objectives of this Act are to safeguard the fundamental rights, freedoms, and interests of data subjects in Nigeria, establish an independent regulatory commission, promote best practices in data processing, and foster safe digital economy growth."
