ABOUT TRUSTSTACK ACADEMY

Learn security by doing.

TrustStack Academy is the learner-facing platform of Truststack Academy and Educational Services Ltd, a Nigerian education company developing practical cybersecurity capability for Africa.

Explore all 8 tracksWork with us

OUR PURPOSE

We help people and organisations develop the practical security capability needed to protect digital services and handle cyber risk.

01

OUR VISION

Capable defenders, learning locally.

An Africa where cyber defenders can develop real skills close to home, prove what they can do and contribute to safer digital systems.

02

OUR MISSION

Accessible, tool-based education.

We deliver guided labs, realistic scenarios, clear feedback and locally relevant pathways that turn cybersecurity concepts into repeatable practice.

HOW LEARNING WORKS

From understanding a concept to proving a skill.

Our learning model centres on the work behind cybersecurity roles. Learners use real tools, complete structured labs and build demonstrable outcomes rather than relying on theory alone.
